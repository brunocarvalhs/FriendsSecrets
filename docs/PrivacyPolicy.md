# Global Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2, 2026

Friends Secrets ("we," "our," or "us"), operated by Brunocarvalhs, provides a platform for organizing "Secret Santa" (Amigo Secreto) groups. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with global data protection regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU/UK), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA - USA), the Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD - Brazil), and other applicable international laws.

---

### 1. Data Controller and Contact Information
Brunocarvalhs is the data controller for the information processed through the App.
**Email:** bruno.silva.carvalho@outlook.com.br

### 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

#### 2.1 Contact Information (Local Processing)
To facilitate the creation of participant groups, the App requests access to your device's contacts.
*   **Data Types:** Names, phone numbers, email addresses, and profile photos.
*   **Legal Basis:** Performance of a contract (providing the requested service).
*   **Processing:** This data is processed **locally on your device**. We do not upload your contact list to our servers, nor is it shared with third parties for their own use.

#### 2.2 Biometric Data (Security)
The App utilizes device-level biometric authentication (e.g., fingerprint, facial recognition) to secure access to your data.
*   **Processing:** Biometric data is managed exclusively by the Android operating system's secure hardware (TEE/SE). We do not access, store, or transmit your raw biometric templates.

#### 2.3 Automated Data Collection (Analytics & Advertising)
We use third-party services that collect data to monitor App performance and provide relevant experiences.
*   **Data Types:** Advertising ID (AD_ID), IP address, device model, OS version, and App usage statistics.
*   **Legal Basis:** Consent (where required) or Legitimate Interest (for service improvement).
*   **Third-Party Providers:**
    *   [Google Play Services](https://www.google.com/policies/privacy/)
    *   [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/policies/analytics)

### 3. Data Retention
We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Since most core data is stored locally, deleting the App or its data through device settings will remove the information from your device.

### 4. International Data Transfers
While core service data is local, analytics data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own (primarily the United States) by our service providers. These transfers are protected by standard contractual clauses or other legal adequacy mechanisms.

### 5. Your Global Privacy Rights
Depending on your location, you have rights regarding your personal data:
*   **Right to Access:** Obtain a copy of your data.
*   **Right to Rectification:** Correct inaccurate data.
*   **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your data.
*   **Right to Restrict Processing:** Object to or limit data processing.
*   **Right to Data Portability:** Receive your data in a structured format.
*   **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent.

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email provided above.

### 6. Children's Privacy
Friends Secrets does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13 (or higher as required by local law). If we become aware of such collection, we will take immediate steps to delete the data.

### 7. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect changes in law or our practices. The "Effective Date" at the top will indicate the latest revision.
